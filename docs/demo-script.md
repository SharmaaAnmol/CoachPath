# CoachPath: 3-5 Minute Hackathon Demo Script
*Build for Bharat Hackathon 2.0*

### 1. Problem Statement (20s)
> *"Judges, over 1.5 million engineering students graduate in India every year, especially from Tier-2 and Tier-3 colleges. They are flooded with generic advice, overwhelmed by job boards, and starved of clear, structured direction. CoachPath changes that. It's not just a job board—it's an end-to-end Career Intelligence & Job Application Assistant."*

### 2. Onboarding (30s)
> *"Let's look at Aarav, a final-year CS undergrad targeting a Machine Learning Engineer role in Bengaluru with a 4-month timeline. Through our 11-step conversational onboarding chat, CoachPath captures Aarav's exact constraints: 20 hours a week, 100% free resources, and target salary in ₹ LPA. As he chats, his profile extracts live in the right sidebar."*

### 3. Assessments & 4-Dimension Rubric (30s)
> *"Unlike tools that trust unchecked claims, CoachPath tests skills across 4 dimensions: Knowledge, Problem Solving, Practical Skills, and Industry Readiness. Let's run a live SQL assessment. In real time, the query evaluates, his level upgrades to Advanced, and our central profile triggers an instant recomputation."*

### 4. Skill Gap & Dynamic Roadmap (30s)
> *"Notice the magic: The roadmap isn't static. It calculated Aarav's 20-hour weekly capacity and sequenced free resources from NPTEL, SWAYAM, and CS50. Because his SQL score just improved, the system automatically re-sequences the roadmap, marking SQL complete and accelerating him toward PyTorch and MLOps."*

### 5. Market Demand & Explainable Job Matching (40s)
> *"Next, our market intelligence engine pulls verified jobs from official APIs like Adzuna and Greenhouse. Looking at Swiggy's Junior ML Engineer posting, Aarav gets a 92% match score. But unlike black-box AI, we show the exact breakdown: 40% skills, 15% semantic alignment, 15% experience, and explainable reasons with checkmarks and trade-offs."*

### 6. Truthful Resume Optimization (40s)
> *"In our Resume Studio, CoachPath generates 3 role-tailored versions. Notice the green banner: 'Nothing was invented. Every line traces to your original resume.' Our dual-pass truthfulness checker guarantees zero hallucinated metrics or employers. The diff view shows exactly what was reworded versus reordered for maximum ATS impact."*

### 7. Human Approval Gate & Recruiter Outreach (40s)
> *"We adhere to a core philosophy: 'Automate the busywork. Never the decision.' When preparing an application for Swiggy, the AI crafts the package, but holds at a Human Approval Gate. Aarav reviews the cover note, clicks Approve, which logs to our immutable Audit Log and opens the official apply link. For recruiters, we draft tight, non-spam cold messages under 90 words with strict rate limits."*

### 8. Tracker & Interview Prep (30s)
> *"The Application Tracker monitors his pipeline with automated 5-day follow-up reminders and one-click CSV export. When Swiggy invites him to interview, CoachPath generates a prep checklist based on his weakest skills, a countdown timer, and an RFC 5545 .ics calendar invite with 3-day, 1-day, and 1-hour alarms."*

### 9. Career Readiness Dashboard (20s)
> *"Finally, our Career Readiness Dashboard synthesizes technical skills, DSA, projects, and interview preparation into a single 70% index. And our mathematical Biggest Lever card tells him exactly: 'Focusing on Core Technical Skills can lift your readiness from 70% to 75%.'"*

### 10. Close & Responsible AI (20s)
> *"With complete data portability, a one-click 'Delete My Data' button, and a Hindi toggle for Bharat students, CoachPath doesn't just help you find a job—it makes you ready for the job. Thank you!"*
